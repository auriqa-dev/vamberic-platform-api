## New Vamberic requirements, not values supplied by HubSpot

Application ID prefixes `pipeline`, `workspace`, `lead`, `task`, `externalref` (interactions reuse `event`); internal actor references; typed field observations; observation confidence and verification; immutable external mapping identity; optimistic updates and reference guards; stable local pipeline stage IDs; target-account and product-specific ICP classification; explicit permission/access boundaries.

The following vocabularies are **Vamberic choices**, not claimed HubSpot option lists:

- Sales lifecycle: `lead`, `qualified`, `opportunity`, `customer`, `advocate`, `disqualified`.
- Lead/outreach status: `new`, `researching`, `ready`, `contacted`, `responded`, `nurture`, `closed`.
- ICP tier: `tier_1`, `tier_2`, `tier_3`, `outside_icp`.
- Priority: `low`, `normal`, `high`, `urgent`; deal type: `new_business`, `renewal`, `expansion`, `other`.
- Buying role: `decision_maker`, `champion`, `economic_buyer`, `technical_buyer`, `influencer`, `end_user`, `blocker`.
- Task status: `pending`, `in_progress`, `completed`, `cancelled`; task type: `call`, `email`, `meeting`, `research`, `follow_up`, `other`.

No historical record is automatically assigned one of these new values. Scores are deferred; any future score must carry scale, method/version, evidence and calculatedAt and be specific to the owning workspace and relevant offering/Product context.

## Deferred and excluded

Deferred object types: order, ticket, invoice, user synchronization, quote, project, payment, line-item, marketing-event synchronization, credit-memo, subscription synchronization, contract and cart. Existing billing remains authoritative and unchanged. No consumer opportunity organisation is fabricated; B2C opportunity identity requires a separate model decision.

Excluded families: HubSpot calculated rollups and weighted revenue, analytics counters, system UI/bookkeeping fields, sequences/agent enrollment, scheduling/repetition controls, email-open tracking, membership/auth state, duplicated task snapshots of contact/company fields, demographic/personal categories unrelated to B2B CRM, Chargebee `cb_*` billing mirrors and product-specific diagnostic questionnaires. Marketing-contact billing status, enrichment availability, discovery and having an email address confer no permission. Exclusion is a scope choice, never inferred from fill rates.

## Ownership review amendment

Client/workspace ownership is a new Vamberic requirement, not a field inferred from this export. Explicit workspace mapping and authorized access must precede any future client import; providerAccountId does not grant ownership or sharing. Existing records without workspaceId remain internal portfolio records only. Client qualification maps to crm_leads; retained product_relationships qualification mappings above describe internal product qualification compatibility. Client campaigns/opportunities/pipelines require workspace context and may optionally link a real Product. Missing client, owner, offer or scope mappings must be reviewed, never fabricated.

Interactions now map exclusively to events.interaction and events.occurredAt; crm_activities was removed before apply. External-reference uniqueness includes workspaceId in addition to provider/account/object/external ID. Source-specific identity strings belong in external_references; structured message/thread references remain interaction evidence. No client records, grants or pipelines are seeded.

The 2026-09-27 architecture review treats Hive as storage, HVMapp/HVM agents as the operational layer and Vapp as oversight through future summaries. HubSpot owner fields may resolve only to an explicitly identified human CRM account owner; agent IDs belong in actor/task/audit metadata, never the owner field. Unknown ownership stays absent. Raw Lead Gen research is upstream of CRM and is not imported merely because a source provides a contact definition; accepted commercial qualification is the promotion boundary.
